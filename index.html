Why we exist

COMSSI was formed by development workers who saw the same pattern again and again. When public money and services do not reach people, young people drop out of school, migrate, or fall into trafficking and political thuggery. Corruption and neglect are usually behind it.

We work from 185 Zaria Road, Kano, with NGOs, community groups and government partners across the country. Our approach is social accountability: giving people the information and skills to ask how public resources are used.

We are registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC/IT/NO81968) and governed by a Board of Trustees. Read more about us.

Our mission

Creating awareness to provide knowledge of self-ability and empower through social accountability.

Our vision

A society where disadvantaged groups have access to quality, affordable education, justice and self-esteem for sustainable development.

Informed citizens get better services. So we start with information.

What we do

Eight areas of work, all built on the same idea: informed citizens get better services. See details and examples.

Budget transparency and civic participation

We help citizens read and question government budgets, expenditure and programmes, and take part in collective advocacy through budget literacy, scorecards and participatory budgeting.

Education and basic literacy

Adult classes, extramural lessons for GCE, SSCE and NECO candidates and school dropouts, and support to reach tertiary institutions.

Health awareness

Reproductive and adolescent health, maternal and child health, HIV/AIDS awareness, and malaria campaigns including the 2022 net distribution in Madobi LGA.

Fighting human trafficking and child abuse

Community awareness on the dangers of trafficking and unsafe migration, and support to children sent to cities for domestic work.

Livelihoods and economic recovery

Small business training, skills acquisition and training of trainers for unemployed youth, women and internally displaced persons in communities affected by insurgency.

Climate-smart agribusiness

Supporting rural agricultural commodity groups to adopt sustainable, climate-smart technologies.

Human rights documentation

Research and publication, including a book documenting human rights violations in Northern Nigeria with Kano and Bauchi as case studies.

Capacity building and training

Training for government officials, CSOs, community and religious leaders on public financial management, budget monitoring, communication and report writing.

A track record with serious partners

15 years of documented work with EU, DFID, Pathfinder and other partners across Kano and Northern Nigeria.
