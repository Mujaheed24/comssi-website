# Hausa translation notes (for the reviewer)
Terms chosen after checking Nigerian Hausa media (Legit Hausa, Aminiya, VOA Hausa, Leadership Hausa), Hausa Wikipedia and Amnesty's Hausa report:
- transparency and accountability = gaskiya da riƙon amana | budget = kasafin kuɗi | public money = kuɗaɗen/dukiyar jama'a
- human trafficking = fataucin mutane | human rights = haƙƙin ɗan Adam | LGA = ƙaramar hukuma (pl. ƙananan hukumomi)
- mosquito net = gidan sauro | malaria = zazzabin cizon sauro | pregnant women = mata masu juna biyu | IDPs = 'yan gudun hijira
- awareness = wayar da kai / wayar da kan jama'a | corruption = cin hanci da rashawa | European Union = Tarayyar Turai
PLEASE CHECK: staff job titles, the organisation's Hausa name (we kept the English name), and "Aikinmu" for "mandate".
Resources/glossary: grant = tallafi | contract = kwangila | NGO = ƙungiya mai zaman kanta | CBO = ƙungiyar al'umma | beneficiaries = masu cin gajiya | volunteering = aikin sa kai.
PLEASE CHECK: "Masu cin gajiya" and "Gudunmawar abokin haɗin gwiwa (Match)" - donors may use different wording.
