Milestones

Selected highlights from our documented work. See the full coalition-building history from 2007–2012.

  1. 2007–2008Trained by the EU SRIP programme in participatory budgeting, public financial management and report writing.
  2. 2008Helped launch COBTAK, the Coalition of Budget Transparency Advocates in Kano, with EU support.
  3. 2009–2012Grew our partnerships nationally, joining NiRTAG and the DFID-funded M4M consortium (see our full history).
  4. 2013–2019Community outreach on insecticidal nets for pregnant women in Tsanyawa, Garko, Sumaila and Gaya LGAs.
  5. 2016Took part in Kano State's Medium Term Sector Strategy and Expenditure Framework planning.
  6. 2022Social mobilisation and net distribution for Kano's malaria elimination campaign in Madobi LGA.
